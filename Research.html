Science Without Limits

The ISS is a unique laboratory where gravity does not interfere with experiments. Scientists from around the world conduct research that is impossible on Earth, leading to breakthroughs that benefit life on our planet.

A Laboratory Like No Other

Over 3,000 research investigations from scientists in more than 108 countries have been conducted aboard the ISS. The microgravity environment allows for experiments that would be impossible on Earth.

Research areas include human health, biology, physics, astronomy, materials science, Earth observation, and technology development.

Biology

Studying how living organisms adapt to microgravity, helping us understand diseases on Earth.

Physics

Conducting experiments impossible on Earth, like observing how fluids behave without gravity.

Material Science

Creating new materials and crystals that cannot be formed under Earth’s gravity.

Human Health

Learning how long duration spaceflight affects the human body for future Mars missions.
