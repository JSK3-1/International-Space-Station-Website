Exploring Life Beyond Earth

The International Space Station is a marvel of human engineering, orbiting Earth at over 17,500 mph while serving as a home for astronauts and a laboratory for groundbreaking research.

Quick Facts

Orbit

Circles Earth every 90 minutes at 250 miles altitude

Size

Larger than a football field at 357 feet long

Crew

Home to 6–7 astronauts from around the world

Launched

First module launched in 1998
